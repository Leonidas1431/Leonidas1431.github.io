Privacy policy

This application is a private, single-user OAuth client used only by its owner to access the owner's own Google Drive through rclone on the owner's own computer.

  • It does not collect, store, sell or share any data on any server.
  • Access tokens are stored only in the owner's local rclone configuration file.
  • No data from Google Drive is sent to third parties.

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
